# Travel reimbursements

Submit travel reimbursements in the expense portal within 45 calendar days of returning. Attach itemized receipts for every claimed expense.

# Hotel limits

The standard overnight hotel cap is EUR 145 per night before local taxes. A regional director may approve a higher amount before travel.

# Rail booking

Book standard class rail unless a documented accessibility need requires a different fare. Use the corporate booking channel for rail journeys.

# Mileage claims

Approved private vehicle trips are reimbursed at EUR 0.42 per kilometre. Record the route and distance in the expense portal.

# Meals and receipts

The meal limit is EUR 45 per day while travelling overnight. Alcohol and minibar items are excluded from reimbursement.
