# Remote work

Employees in eligible shore roles may work remotely up to two days each week. The team lead approves the days in the shared team calendar.

# Core availability

Remote staff must be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local office time. Use the team chat status to indicate a planned absence during those hours.

# Home office equipment

The service desk issues a headset and laptop stand on request. A manager must preauthorize purchases made outside the equipment catalogue.

# Desk booking

Reserve a desk in the office booking app by 17:00 on the previous working day. Cancel unneeded bookings before 09:00 on the day.

# Remote access

Company files may be accessed only from managed laptops using the approved VPN. Report suspected device loss by phone to the service desk immediately.
