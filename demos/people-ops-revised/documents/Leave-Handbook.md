# Annual leave

Permanent shore employees receive 25 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Carryover is capped at five days and expires on 31 March of the next year.

# Leave notice

Submit ordinary leave requests in the PeopleHub portal at least ten working days before departure. A line manager must approve the request before booking travel.

# Sick leave

Notify your line manager before 09:30 local time on the first day of sickness. A medical certificate is required after three consecutive working days.

# Parental leave

Applications for planned parental leave should reach People Operations at least eight weeks before the expected start. Managers must confirm the handover plan separately.

# Public holidays

The local office calendar determines public holidays for shore employees. If a holiday falls during approved annual leave, the holiday does not reduce the annual leave balance.

# Compassionate leave

Employees can request up to three paid working days after the death of an immediate family member. People Operations reviews requests for extra days.
