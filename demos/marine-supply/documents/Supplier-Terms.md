# Supplier returns

Open an RMA request within 21 calendar days of delivery for unused parts. Include the purchase order and an image of the original packaging.

# Warranty claims

Warranty claims are accepted for manufacturing defects within 12 months of dispatch. Include the serial number and a description of operating conditions.

# Restocking charges

An accepted discretionary return incurs a 12 percent restocking charge. There is no restocking charge on factory defects.

# Damaged consignments

Note obvious damage on the delivery receipt and send photos to the supplier within two working days. Keep the outer packaging until the carrier releases it.

# Stock reservations

A confirmed stock reservation lasts five business days. The supplier releases reserved items when the reservation expires without a purchase order.
