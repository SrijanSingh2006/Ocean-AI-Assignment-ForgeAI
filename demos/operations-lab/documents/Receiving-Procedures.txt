# Receiving procedures

Match the packing list against the shipment ID before opening the pallet. Record unresolved quantity differences in the receiving log.

# Temperature check

Inspect refrigerated packages at the dock. Reject cartons with a measured surface temperature above 8 degrees Celsius and record the observed reading.

# Shipping handover

Record the driver's full name and vehicle plate in the shift log. Ask the driver to sign the handover receipt.
