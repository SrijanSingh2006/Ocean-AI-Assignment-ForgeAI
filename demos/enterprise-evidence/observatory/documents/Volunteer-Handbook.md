# Night shift check-in

Volunteers sign in at the north lobby 20 minutes before their shift. The shift lead checks the radio battery before granting roof access.

# Public telescope reservations

Visitors reserve a telescope through the visitor desk. A reservation lasts 30 minutes and accommodates four visitors.

# Red-light rules

Use red light on the observation deck. White lights stay off after 19:00 unless an evacuation is in progress.
